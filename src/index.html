About Bayshore

Homecare Services For New York

Healing from Surgery, recovering from illness, or dealing with the inevitable aging of a loved one can test any family. But major life challenges shouldn’t change how you and they live. We help your loved ones live happier, healthier lives in their long-term homes. From short-term, in-home care to round-the-clock nursing services, their health, dignity, and happiness are always at the forefront of our decisions.

As an owner-run, New York State Department of Health Licensed Home Care Services Agency (LHCSA), we've been proudly serving the Long Island and Queens community since 2015. Here at Bayshore, we blend advanced clinical expertise with a compassionate approach, fostering a supportive atmosphere for our staff while ensuring our clients can easily connect with us whenever they need assistance.
