About Us

Caring for Your Loved Ones with Love!

Healing from surgery, recovering from illness, or dealing with the inevitable aging of a loved one can test any family. But significant life challenges shouldn’t change how you and they live. We help your loved ones enjoy a high quality of life in their long-term homes. From short-term, in-home care to round-the-clock nursing services, our client’s health, dignity, and happiness are always at the forefront of any decisions we make.

Always There for You:

For geriatric and pediatric clients, Bayshore Home Healthcare has been providing quality care to the Long Island and Queens community since 2015. We are proud of the reputation we have built over the years, and our management team is well-equipped to meet the demands of dynamic environments.

Advanced and Compassionate Care

Here at Bayshore, we blend advanced clinical expertise with a compassionate approach. We balance a highly supportive atmosphere for our staff while ensuring our clients can easily connect with us whenever they need assistance. Our mission has been and always will be to provide the highest quality of care and service at an affordable price.
